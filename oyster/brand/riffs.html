Fan and pearl: riffs

Eight variations on B1 and B3, the front-on fan, dish and pearl from the second reference. Each changes one idea: what the ribs are made of, the fan’s edge, the pearl, or how the mark sits as the o of the logotype. B1 and B3 are shown first. Each option is shown in both palettes, as the o of the logotype, at real pixel sizes and in one colour.
