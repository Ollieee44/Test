Blobbier proteins

Round twelve’s 02, with blobbier proteins. Each protein’s own traced lobes are amplified, measured against its overall oval so only the local bulges grow, and clefts are pressed into its back; some get a wider domain at one end. They are then docked as before: each holds half the pearl, the facing sides fit at a hairline gap, and the surfaces are molecular. Structures: PDB 5T35, Gadd et al., Nat. Chem. Biol. 2017; the degrader is drawn generically.
