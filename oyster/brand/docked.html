Docked

Round seven’s 02, with the proteins brought together. BRD4 (the lid) and VHL (the cup) now fit each other: their facing surfaces are reshaped to follow one another an even gap apart, as docked proteins do, with the same gap round the pearl. Their backs keep the shapes traced from PDB 5T35, so they still read as protein outlines. The variations change how far they dock, the gap, the view and the mouth. Structures: PDB 5T35, Gadd et al., Nat. Chem. Biol. 2017; the degrader is drawn generically.
