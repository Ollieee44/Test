Oyster Therapeutics · brandmark brainstorm, round five

The mark is the molecule

Your brief: a stylised bifunctional with the halves of an oyster in the middle of the linker, and the pearl as the secret sauce. So each mark is a degrader: a different ligand at each end (a heterobifunctional’s ends differ; both are invented ring systems, per the generic-degrader rule), the linker between them, and at its middle the two valves of an oyster with the pearl cupped between them as the reversible clasp. Split, the valves part and the pearl parts with them, each valve keeping half. The valves round the pearl make a loose O. The pearl is in the logo’s pearl colour.

01

Bracketed

The degrader laid straight: a different ligand at each end, and in the middle of the linker the two valves facing each other round the pearl, like brackets: ( • ). The valves and pearl make the O.

The clearest read of a bifunctional, and the shell-and-pearl O sits right where the o goes.

Lid and cup

Curled

The whole degrader bent into an O: the linker runs from one ligand round to the other, and at its middle is the oyster, two small valves cupping the pearl. The two ligands nearly meet, closing the O.
