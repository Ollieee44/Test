Our science

Getting protein degradation into the brain

Targeted protein degradation removes a disease-causing protein instead of blocking it. The hard part in neurology is delivery. We design for it from the first molecule.

Oral Brain-penetrant Degrader
01

Taken as a pill

Our molecules are optimised for oral absorption, so treatment can be a daily tablet at home rather than an infusion in a clinic.

02

Crosses into the brain

Degraders are usually large molecules that the blood-brain barrier keeps out. We tune size, shape and polarity so ours reach meaningful levels in brain tissue.

03

Removes the target protein

Once inside the neuron, the degrader tags the disease-causing protein for the cell's own disposal system, which breaks it down and clears it.
