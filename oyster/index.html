Discover SELFTAC®
Vision

We are building a pipeline of medicines on one idea: turn large protein degraders into small molecules, so targeted protein degradation can reach patients as oral drugs, including in the brain.

Our approach
Approach

Two small molecules. One degrader, assembled where it is needed.

The size problem

Bifunctional degraders are versatile and can be designed rationally, but their large size gives poor drug-like properties.

A reversible linker

SELFTAC® places a reversible linker between the two halves, turning one large degrader into two small molecules.

Small enough to travel

Small molecules are far easier to give orally, and can cross the blood-brain barrier that protects the central nervous system.

Self-assembly at the target

Inside the cell the two halves recombine into the full degrader, which tags the disease-causing protein for removal.

Why Oyster

Something small, built into something remarkable.

A pearl forms inside the shell. SELFTAC® molecules work in a similar way: they enter as small drugs and assemble into a degrader inside the cell.

Pipeline

Drug discovery is underway in oncology and diseases of the central nervous system, reflecting the broad opportunity of protein degradation in chronic disease.
