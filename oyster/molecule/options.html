Drawing the degrader

Four static treatments for the molecule in the SELFTAC story, replacing the circle and square. Each is drawn from a real 2D layout of a bifunctional degrader: the BRD4 binder on the left, the E3-ligase binder on the right, and the linker between them, emphasised and broken at its reversible bond.

The geometry is MZ1 (JQ1, a PEG linker and VH032), the PROTAC in PDB 5T35. It stands in for "a degrader" and is not a SELFTAC compound. The break point is placed at the middle of the linker for illustration only.
