A new landing page, desktop first. The sequence plays as you scroll: one screen-filling scene, with short lines of copy fading in beside it. Times are at an easy scrolling pace.

The degrader shown is MZ1, the molecule in the 5T35 crystal structure, standing in for a SELFTAC compound. The proteins are smoothed molecular surfaces of the real chains, with atomic detail removed on purpose; the degrader is generic too: each half is a smooth outline traced along its bonds, glossy rather than contoured so it never reads as another protein.
